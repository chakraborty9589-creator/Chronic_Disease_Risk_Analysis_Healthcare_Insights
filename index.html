Worksheet 11: Key Analytical Findings & Evaluation Synthesis

Defensible observations calculated directly from the underlying dataset records.

Verified Analytics
1. Highest Observed Prevalence

Diabetes holds the highest overall prevalence in the cohort at 34.92% (1,746 patients), followed by Heart Disease at 29.98% (1,499 patients) and Stroke at 24.86% (1,243 patients).

2. Determinant Age-Tier Dynamics

Patients under 50 have 0.0% observed disease prevalence in this dataset. Disease rates begin in ages 50–59 (HD: 8.6%, Diabetes: 15.2%), spike dramatically in 60–69 (HD: 74.8%, Diabetes: 98.7%, Stroke: 51.6%), and reach 100% saturation in patients ≥ 70.

3. BMI Risk Gradient

Heart disease exhibits a marked upward gradient across BMI strata: 20.5% in Underweight (<18.5) → 26.0% in Normal Weight → 32.9% in Overweight → 40.0% in Obesity (≥30.0).

Methodological Limitations & Cautions
  • Cross-sectional observational dataset: observed associations do not imply medical causation.
  • Zero patient identifier exists in raw data; patient entity integrity preserved.
  • Clinical indicator distributions (cholesterol, glucose, BP) show overlapping interquartile ranges between disease cohorts.
